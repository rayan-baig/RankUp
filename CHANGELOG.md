## 0.25.0 — 2026-11-21

Fixes from a strict re-review: the right pay on Home, a picture for every world, a safer parent PIN, and plainer words.

- **"Do this next" shows the real pay.** The card at the top of a child's Home worked out its own number from the bare XP, so an Elite child read "+30 XP · +6" there and "+45 XP · +9" on the same quest's card. Home, the quest cards and the quest's own page now use one calculation (`questPay` in src/lib/xp.js): the Elite boost, a surprise double and the child's streak included, and a race-the-clock bonus only once the clock is beaten.
- **A picture for every world.** Fourteen of the fifteen worlds showed the same empty hooded figure on the theme picker and as the first avatar. Each now has its own original drawing in its own colours: a friendly cube robot (MatrixBlocks), a squad leader in an orange-striped helmet (Tactical Override), a sky-diver with goggles under a glider (Apex Royale), a hero with a red headband in a golden aura (Aura Awakening), a girl with a big pink bow among pixel hearts (Coquette Circuit), a singer under two spotlights (Pop Arena), a winged horse among the clouds (Pegasus Horizon), a baker in a chef's hat with a cupcake (Sugar Rush), a stylist under a ring of mirror lights (Glam Protocol), a camper in a knitted hat by the fire and the tents (Campfire Cozy), a visor on a neon grid at sunset (Cyber Grid), an explorer in a gold star helmet by a little castle (Plumber Kingdom), headphones among neon triangles and an equaliser (Neon Pulse), and a round blue companion with a star tail (Monster Tamer). Each is a small file (about 2 KB) fetched only when it is on the screen. Block Craft keeps the owner's own nine pictures, unchanged, and its card now shows the first of them.
- **The owner's pictures win.** RankUp's drawings live in `src/assets/drawn/`. A file with the same name dropped into `src/assets/art/` (say `avatar-pegasus.png` from an image generator) replaces the drawing, with nothing else to change. `src/assets/art/README.md` says how.
- **The parent PIN is no longer stored as typed, and can't be guessed.** The phone used to keep `"pin":"4321"` in its storage. It now keeps a salted hash and never the PIN. A phone saved by an older version has its PIN moved over the moment the app opens (and on the first unlock at the latest), and the plain value is removed. After 5 wrong tries every PIN box (Who's playing?, the theme lock, Theme Genesis) waits 1 minute and says so calmly ("Too many wrong PINs. Take a breather and try again in 1 minute."); each wait after that is twice as long, up to a day, and a reload doesn't start the count again. It all runs on the phone, so it works with no signal. A PIN reset proved with the account password ends the wait. Settings now changes the PIN with a Save button (it used to save every digit as it was typed) and shows an empty box, because there is no PIN to show.
- **Words that were out of date.** The first-week list says "In Settings, Link a kid's device, tap “Code for Mia”" (the code is made on the parent's phone now). The leaderboards only mention guilds while guilds are switched on. Setup's first step no longer mentions "the guild". The demo no longer shows the developer note about `?device=kid`.
- **Arcade tiles.** Every game has its own simple tile instead of a few shared ones, and the NEW badge is only on a game that is really new to the arcade (none is, today), not on every game a child hasn't played.
- **"Sent!"** A short moment on the quest list after a child taps Send to parent, naming the chore. It goes by itself after about two seconds.
- **Easier to read.** The difficulty chip ("Medium") and the XP box ("+45 XP") are drawn in the difficulty's colour pulled toward the world's ink, at least 4.5:1 on all fifteen worlds. Other coloured chips get the same treatment.
- **Headers follow the world.** The night-city picture behind the Quests, Rewards, Arcade and You headers showed on every world, pastel ones included. On a dark world it is now tinted in that world's colours; on a light world the header is the world's own lit gradient.
- **Plans.** An Elite family's Starter card said "Stay on Starter"; each card's button now says which way it goes ("Move to Starter · free", "Move down to Standard", "Switch to Elite"). Moving to the free plan while paying opens the same steps as "Cancel my plan". The arcade's daily winnings are given in the word the family's children see ("up to 15 Gems a day"), not "coins".
- **Plurals.** "3 quests assigned", "1 chore", and the same for the other counts that said "quest(s)" or "1 chores".
- Also: "Forgot your PIN?" loads when it is tapped, which keeps the first file inside its budget with the PIN code added.
- **Before deploying:** nothing to run on the database and no new setting.
- Tests: new `tests/fixes25.mjs` (static: the pay agreement, the drawings and their sizes, the PIN hash, the move-over and the waits against node:crypto and the clock, the words, the contrast on every world, plurals) and `tests/fixes25ui.mjs` (smoke: the three pay displays agree in the demo, every world card and arcade tile has its own picture, no NEW badge, the headers, "Sent!", Plans, then a new family's PIN: hash only, moved over, the waits across reloads, a new PIN from Settings). Updated: `tests/planwords.mjs` (the children's word, the plan buttons), `tests/pinreset.mjs` (the hash, not the PIN), `tests/speed.mjs` (drawings are never written into the code).

## 0.24.0 — 2026-11-20

Pilot, the MTRX Horizyn AI helper, for parents: in Parent Mode only, on the parent's own AI key.

- **Pilot in Parent Mode.** After the PIN, a small robot sits above the tab bar on every parent screen. Tap it for Pilot's window ("Your AI copilot. Powered by you."), the same robot and window as on the MTRX Horizyn site (the shared Pilot core 1.1.0, copied unchanged with its 18 pose pictures). It is also on the signed-out front door and the gift and School League pages, for parents and visitors.
- **The moving robot.** Once the page is idle the robot breathes, blinks and now and then strolls, waves or does a trick, only where Pilot itself is: it lives inside Pilot, so it is never anywhere a child can see, and it is sent home and kept still whenever Pilot is put away. It keeps still, and is not even downloaded, under the phone's "reduce motion" or RankUp's own Reduce motion setting. It steps out of the way while one of RankUp's pop-ups is open.
- **Never for a child.** Pilot is not on any child's screen, the demo's Ava and Noah views included, and not on a child's phone at all: one set up, one being set up, or one unlinked from being a child's. It goes the moment Parent Mode is left ("Hand to a kid", the PIN screen) and comes back with the PIN. Two locks, both checked: the screen must be one of Pilot's, and nothing in the app's state may say a child could be holding the phone. A child's phone never even downloads Pilot, and anything Pilot once kept on it is deleted.
- **The parent's own key.** Pilot works with a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or a compatible service, which the parent adds in Pilot's settings. The key stays in that browser (for this visit only, unless "Remember my keys on this device" is ticked). RankUp's server never sees the key or the conversation and pays nothing for it. Signing out, deleting the account or leaving the demo forgets the chat, Pilot's memory notes and any key kept for this visit.
- **What it knows.** How RankUp works, written from the app itself: chores, Review, XP, levels, coins, rewards, pocket money and the money sign, linking a phone with a code, Pause and limits, the plans with the prices from the plan list, gifts, Flash Tickets, the Sunday Market, the deals and Seasons from 0.23.0, and privacy. It also has the parent help page's answers word for word, and a short, honest "why RankUp" list from real features.
- **What it can do.** Answer "what's waiting for review", "how is Ava doing this week", "what rewards are set up", "explain this screen" and "what does Elite add". With a card first ("Pilot wants to: add the chore “Feed the dog” for Ava (30 XP)", Allow or Cancel): add a chore, accept a finished chore, send one back with a note, add a reward, add the three starter chores. Each goes through the same action as the parent's own button, so it is saved and synced the same way. Pilot can't change the plan or buy anything (Flash Tickets included), set or lift a pause or limit, take coins, delete anything, remove a child, link or unlink a phone, or message anyone.
- **What is sent to the AI.** Only when the parent sends a message, and only to the company they chose: their message, RankUp's guide, the screen's name and a few counts. Nothing about a child unless the parent types it or asks Pilot to look something up or change something, and then only first names and chore titles: never an age, a photo, a phone or anything a child typed. Children's names on the parent screens are marked private, so "Explain this screen" leaves them out, and any family name still on the screen is taken out too.
- **The privacy policy and terms** describe Pilot: parents only, the parent's own key, what is and isn't sent, and that the AI company bills the parent. The consent notice is unchanged (still 2026-12): RankUp collects nothing new, since Pilot runs in the parent's browser on the parent's own request and RankUp keeps none of it (docs/LEGAL.md).
- **Security policy.** `connect-src` adds exactly Pilot's AI providers (the MTRX site's list, and `http://localhost:*` and `http://127.0.0.1:*` for a model on the parent's own computer), `media-src` adds `blob:` (a video Pilot made), and the Permissions-Policy allows the microphone for this site only (Pilot's voice button). The policy is still only in the page (vite.config.js); `public/_headers` and `vercel.json` carry the Permissions-Policy.
- **Still fast.** The first file grows by Pilot's small gate only (about 0.7 KB compressed). Everything else loads after the page has finished loading, and only where Pilot is allowed; the service worker never fetches Pilot ahead of time.
- **Before deploying:** nothing to run on the database and no new setting.
- Tests: new `tests/pilot.mjs` (static: the core's own tests in `tests/js/pilot.test.js`, the core's version, the policy's hosts, lazy loading, no model names, private names, the privacy policy), `tests/pilottools.mjs` (awards: the tools through the real reducer, confirm before every change, every forbidden action refused, what reaches the AI, where Pilot is and is not in every device state) and `tests/pilotui.mjs` (smoke: the robot, the window, a confirm card, gone on every child's screen and a child's phone in every state). `tests/bundle.mjs` (the policy and the first file's budget) and `tests/isolation.mjs` (Pilot's hosts) updated; `tests/launchscreens.mjs`, `tests/layout.mjs` and `tests/schoolsignup.mjs` no longer count Pilot's robot button as a help question or a closed section; `tests/oldbrowsers.mjs` knows Pilot's config checks for BroadcastChannel first.

## 0.23.0 — 2026-11-20

Deals for parents, and Seasons that run themselves.

- **Founding Families.** The first families on a yearly plan (500 unless the setting says otherwise) get their first year at a founding price, through a Stripe coupon. The plan screen says the percent, the places left and the real end date: the places left are counted in the database (a place is held when the checkout starts, made the family's when Stripe's signed webhook says it was paid, and given back if the checkout closes unpaid), never made up, and the last place is never given twice. One per family, never on top of the half-price month, and it ends when the places run out or its last day passes.
- **The holiday gift sale.** Gift codes a percentage off between two days set in the settings (for example Black Friday to 31 December), taken off at the payment page. Parents see it on the dashboard and on the Give page, with its end date. Off when the settings are empty.
- **Monthly to yearly, keeping what you paid.** After a family has paid two months, "Switch to yearly" says "keep what you have paid": Stripe's plan-change page takes what is left of the month off the year and shows it before anything is charged.
- **The plan screen says the saving.** "Save 30% yearly" (and "months free" when a year costs at least two months less) on each paid plan, worked out from the plan list's own prices, and "Standard or Elite?", line by line.
- **"Gift a year", a month in.** After 30 days a family's dashboard asks, once, whether they know a family who would like a year of RankUp; "Not now" hides it for good.
- **Seasons run themselves.** The owner sets launch day once in the operator page. Season 1 starts that day, and each Season starts 4 months after the one before. Every day the autopilot makes the next Season a month ahead (secret until its first day), and puts in it the themes the owner has queued ("Queue for next Season", any time). A theme a child made is never published without the owner's pick. If nothing is queued the Season still starts, with its event and its deal, and the owner gets an email seven days before. On the day, every child's app shows "New Season!" by itself. It runs from pg_cron where that is switched on, and from the daily clean-up job either way.
- **The Season's deal.** For the first 14 days of each Season, the yearly plan (or gift codes) a percentage off, from the settings, with its real end date. Parents only.
- **Season 1, the Founding Season.** It starts on launch day. Children earn the **Founding Season badge** by doing 10 chores during Season 1 (never bought; kept for good), and see a Season 1 banner on their home screen with how many they have done, with no countdown. "New Season!" says what the badge is. The Founding Families deal starts the same day; the holiday gift sale keeps its own dates.
- **docs/SEASONS.md** is new: the launch date (`Launch date: not yet` until launch day), the rule, and a table of the Seasons built, in a shape a monthly task reads to build the next Seasons. The top of the file says how.
- **Never on a child's phone.** None of the deals, prices or offers is shown on a child's screens, and a child's phone never even asks for them. No countdowns and no made-up scarcity anywhere. No price changed: every discount is a Stripe coupon whose id is a setting.
- Checkouts for plans and gifts now carry Stripe idempotency keys, so a second tap or a retry is the same checkout.
- **Before deploying:** run `supabase/billing.sql` and `supabase/sync.sql` again on the live database, in that order (new tables `founding_places`, `season_plan`, `season_reminders`; `theme_submissions` can be "queued"; new server-only functions, all revoked from browsers; `billing_status` gains `paid_months`). ## 0.21.0 — 2026-11-19

Fast and solid: a lighter first load on a slow phone, no request that can hang for ever, and health checks a free monitor can watch.

- **The first screen arrives sooner on a slow phone.** Measured on a simulated slow 4G phone (docs/PERFORMANCE.md): the front door's first paint went from about 1.6 to about 1.5 seconds, and a second visit's from about 1.0 to about 0.5 seconds, with 1.5 KB downloaded instead of 33 KB.
  - **The typefaces come from RankUp's own site, not Google.** The same three fonts at the same weights (every screen was compared pixel for pixel), so nothing looks different. The page no longer waits on a stylesheet from another company before it can draw anything, it no longer goes blank on a network that blocks Google, the fonts now open offline, and a child's phone no longer tells Google it opened RankUp.
  - **The first file a phone downloads is a third smaller** (337 KB to 236 KB): the sixty-odd avatars, arcade tiles and icons were written into it as text, for screens most visits never open. Each is now its own file, fetched when shown. The arcade's tiles and the theme picker's pictures load as they scroll into view. Block Craft's avatar pictures are unchanged (already small: 13 to 21 KB each, no hidden extras to strip).
  - **Files that never change are kept for a year.** Everything in `assets/` has its contents' fingerprint in its name, so phones now keep it without asking again; before, a second visit asked the server about each of about twenty files.
- **Nothing waits for ever.** Every call the server makes to the database now gives up after 10 seconds (25 for a scheduled job), as Stripe, email and push already did; a slow Supabase gives a parent the usual plain message instead of a spinner, and a job records the failure where `/api/ready` shows it. A phone's sync request gives up after 30 seconds and tries again later like a dropped signal: before, one stuck request on a weak signal stopped every pull and change behind it until the app was closed.
- **Health checks.** `/api/healthz` says the host is up and asks nothing else. `/api/health` is unchanged: up and reaching the database. New `/api/ready` is 503 when anything needs you: a scheduled job gone quiet, a required setting missing (named, never its value, with the `CRON_SECRET`), or a deleted family still waiting for Stripe. All three answer only `{ ok, version }` to anybody, accept HEAD, and keep their answer 15 seconds so a monitor costs the database almost nothing. **docs/OPERATIONS.md** is new: which address to give a free uptime monitor, and "If the app is down" in plain steps.
- **The database's slow spots, at 50,000 families.** The adult list in Settings read every parent of every family (half a second); now 1 ms. The weekly digest job worked out every quiet family's whole week before finding it had nothing to say (7 seconds a call, close to the database's limit for one request); quiet families are now skipped with one look at an index (1.5 seconds). Deleting a reward or a family no longer reads every purchase or every pocket-money entry.
- **Before deploying:** run `supabase/schema.sql`, `supabase/allowance.sql` and `supabase/digests.sql` again on the live database, in that order (three new indexes, made with `create index if not exists`, and `claim_due_digests_at` changed). No new table, no new function, no new setting. Then set up the uptime monitors in docs/OPERATIONS.md.
- Tests: new `tests/failsoft.mjs` and `tests/speed.mjs` (static); `tests/bundle.mjs` checks the first file's size and that no picture or third-party stylesheet is in the build; `tests/api.mjs` covers `/api/healthz` and `/api/ready`; `supabase/test/18-upgrade.sql` (the new indexes), `34-scheduled-jobs.sql` (no quiet family's week is worked out) and `36-hot-queries.sql` (the adult list uses its index). Each new check fails on 0.20.2.
